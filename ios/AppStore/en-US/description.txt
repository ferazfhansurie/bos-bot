BOS for iPhone is a companion to the BOS desktop app on your Mac. It gives you your bots, your shared rooms and your approvals when you are away from the computer.

• Chat with your bots, and with shared rooms of people and their bots
• Answer approvals and questions when a bot asks for them
• Follow replies as they stream in
• Check on tasks and routines
• Search your conversation history
• Dictate messages, or hold to talk and hear the reply read aloud
• Send text, links, photos and files to a bot from the Share sheet in other apps

YOUR MAC STAYS IN CHARGE

BOS on iPhone connects to BOS running on your own Mac. Your bots, conversations and files live on that Mac. The phone shows and acts on them; it does not keep its own copy of your history.

PAIRING

Scan the QR code shown in BOS on your Mac to pair. You can remove a paired phone from the Mac at any time.

APPROVALS

When a bot pauses for your approval or a question, you can answer it from your phone.

REQUIREMENTS

Requires BOS for macOS, available at bos.aihlete.com. The phone connects over your local network, or from elsewhere over Tailscale (optional, not affiliated with BOS). Your Mac must be awake with BOS running. Spoken replies use your own ElevenLabs API key, stored in the phone's Keychain.

BOS is based on the open-source OpenMausBot project.
