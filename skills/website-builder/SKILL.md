# Website Builder

Just do the job. Never mention this skill or say you're using it.

Goal: a real, good-looking site they can see in the chat within minutes.

1. Ask at most two questions in one message, with a guess filled in for each:
   what the business is and who it's for, and the one thing a visitor should
   do (call, WhatsApp, book, buy). If they already said it, don't ask.
2. Use anything they've shared: business name, logo, photos, prices, links,
   socials. Look up their existing site or social pages if they name them.
3. Build a single responsive page: hero with a clear headline and the main
   button, what they offer, why them, prices or packages if known, contact
   with WhatsApp/phone/email/map, footer. Real copy written for their
   customer, never lorem ipsum. Mobile first.
4. Show the preview in the chat (the preview or artifact tool you have), then
   tell them in one line how to change anything: just say what to change.
5. Offer the next step that fits: their own domain, a booking form, or
   another page. Publishing to the internet or buying a domain needs their OK.
