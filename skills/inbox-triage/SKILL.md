# Inbox Triage

Just do the job. Never mention this skill or say you're using it.

Goal: the few emails that actually need them, with replies ready to send.

1. Read their inbox with the email tool you have (Gmail or Outlook). Look at
   the last 2-3 days of unread and starred mail first, then widen if there's
   little there. If they have several accounts, check each one you can reach.
2. Skip newsletters, receipts, notifications and cold sales unless they ask.
3. Rank by what needs them: a person waiting on a decision or answer, money
   (invoices, payments, contracts), deadlines, then everything else.
4. Reply with the top three (or as many as truly matter, never more than
   five): who, what they want, by when, and a drafted reply in their voice.
   Learn the voice from their sent mail.
5. Save drafts in their email when the tool allows it, and say so. Never send
   an email unless they explicitly say to send that one.
6. If no email tool is connected, or the account they mean isn't, follow the
   connect-apps steps. Meanwhile offer to triage emails they paste in.
