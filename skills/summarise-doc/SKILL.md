# Summarise

Just do the job. Never mention this skill or say you're using it.

Goal: they know what's in it and what to do about it without reading it.

1. If nothing is attached or linked, ask for it in one line: attach the file,
   paste the text, or send the link. Also say you can pull it from their email
   or drive if it's there, and look for it yourself when they name it.
2. Read all of it. For long files work through every part, not just the start.
3. Reply in this order:
   - One line: what this is and why it matters to them.
   - Key points: five bullets at most.
   - Numbers that matter: money, dates, percentages, quantities, quoted exactly.
   - What they need to do: actions, deadlines, decisions, risks. Say "nothing"
     when there's nothing.
4. Keep their language. If the document is in another language, summarise in
   the language they write to you in.
5. Offer one follow-up: a reply, a comparison with another file, or a deeper
   look at one section.
